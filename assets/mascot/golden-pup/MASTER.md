# Golden Pup Mascot Master

Status: mascot type approved; canonical pose assets not approved yet.

Golden Pup is the small golden retriever puppy companion in G_QMark Visual.

## Stable identity

- unmistakably a young golden retriever puppy
- compact round body and slightly oversized head
- short legs and simplified paws
- floppy rounded ears
- broad cream muzzle and small dark nose
- gentle, slightly sleepy eyes
- softly curved simplified tail
- warm golden-ochre coat with cream muzzle and chest
- graphite rounded outline

It must feel young, soft, clumsy, affectionate and curious—not like an adult dog or a generic cartoon animal.

## Drawing language

Match the small Wenhao stick figure:

- rounded, slightly imperfect graphite doodle lines
- sparse warm flat fill
- minimal fur detail
- simple silhouette readable at small video-overlay size
- no realistic texture, lighting or anatomy

## Role

Golden Pup may represent companionship, curiosity, waiting, observation, mild confusion, progress and completion. It is not a robot assistant and does not need to appear in every scene.

## Default actions

1. sitting quietly
2. lying with chin on paws
3. following Wenhao
4. carrying a soft ball
5. peeking from a folder
6. beside a laptop or NAS
7. watching a progress bar
8. near a camera
9. mild confusion
10. pleased / complete
11. sleepy / waiting
12. interacting with Wenhao

## Production

Generate isolated poses with generous padding and export approved assets with alpha transparency.

Recommended files:

- `golden-pup-neutral-v01.png`
- `golden-pup-resting-v01.png`
- `golden-pup-waiting-v01.png`
- `golden-pup-peeking-v01.png`
- `golden-pup-complete-v01.png`

Do not silently change the golden retriever breed cues, puppy proportions, floppy ears, cream muzzle, warm coat or minimal doodle style.

# Wenhao Small Stick-Figure Master

Status: character type approved; canonical pose assets not approved yet.

Wenhao is the small line-drawn human narrator in G_QMark Visual.

## Stable identity

- adult male represented as a tiny stick figure
- slightly oversized simple round head
- short black hair drawn with only a few strokes
- tiny simple glasses
- calm, slightly sleepy eyes and restrained mouth
- very short line body, arms and legs
- small dark torso shape suggesting a black T-shirt
- one tiny warm-yellow pendant dot
- total height around 2.5–3 head diameters

The figure must feel small, compact and friendly. Never stretch it into a tall or anatomically realistic stick figure.

## Drawing language

- graphite linework near `#30343B`
- rounded, slightly imperfect editorial doodle strokes
- minimal fills and no rendered anatomy
- tiny mitten-like hands and rounded feet
- props simplified to the same line language
- enough line weight to survive video scaling

Avoid detailed faces, clothing folds, fingers, shoe details, anime rendering, chibi painting, glossy shading and 3D.

## Default actions

Create reusable isolated poses for:

1. neutral
2. thinking
3. laptop / coding
4. reading a document
5. holding a camera
6. holding a small tool or board
7. pointing left
8. pointing right
9. presenting
10. mild confusion
11. realization
12. relaxed / complete

## Interaction with Golden Pup

Wenhao is the narrator and decision-maker. Golden Pup adds companionship, curiosity and process reactions. Neither should become large enough to overpower the information.

## Production

Generate isolated poses with generous padding, preserve the glasses and pendant, then export approved assets with alpha transparency.

Recommended files:

- `wenhao-stick-neutral-v01.png`

- `wenhao-stick-laptop-v01.png`

Do not silently change the short proportions, round head, glasses, sparse hair, dark torso or yellow pendant.

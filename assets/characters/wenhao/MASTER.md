# Wenhao Character Master

Status: provisional specification, no canonical character sheet approved yet.

This file defines the stable identity features of the recurring Wenhao character. Generated images are references only until an approved master sheet is added to this directory.

## 1. Role in the visual system

Wenhao represents the narrator / maker / investigator in personal videos and explainers.

Use the character when a human action helps explain the content: inspecting, comparing, testing, building, filming, reading, coding, presenting or reacting to a result.

Do not insert the character merely to fill empty space.

## 2. Stable identity features

Keep these features consistent across all approved variants:

- adult male
- short black hair
- simple glasses with dark thin frames
- calm, friendly facial expression
- dark charcoal or black top
- small warm-yellow / gold pendant as a subtle recurring detail
- simplified editorial illustration proportions rather than realistic portrait proportions

The character should be recognizable through the combination of glasses, hair, dark top and small gold accent, not through photorealistic facial likeness.

## 3. Proportions

Preferred direction:

- head slightly enlarged for readability
- total body height around 3.5–4 head units
- compact torso and limbs
- hands simplified but able to hold tools or point clearly
- feet and shoes simple, without fashion detail

Avoid both extremes:

- do not make the character a stick figure
- do not make the character an oversized-head chibi / anime mascot

## 4. Face

- minimal eyes and mouth
- glasses remain clearly visible at small size
- nose may be omitted or represented with a tiny simple line
- expression range should remain restrained

Useful expressions:

- neutral
- thinking
- focused
- mild confusion
- realization
- satisfied / task complete
- explaining

Avoid exaggerated crying, rage, shock, meme faces or highly theatrical anime expressions.

## 5. Hair

- black or near-black
- short, tidy silhouette
- no detailed individual strands required
- keep the outer shape consistent across poses

The silhouette matters more than texture.

## 6. Clothing

Default outfit:

- dark charcoal / black T-shirt or simple crew-neck top
- minimal seams
- no large printed logo
- small gold/yellow pendant

Contextual variants may add a jacket, workwear, camera strap or practical accessory if the scene requires it, but the base silhouette should remain recognizable.

## 7. Line and fill treatment

- graphite outlines rather than pure black when possible
- confident clean lines with mild hand-drawn character
- flat or lightly textured fills
- very limited shadow
- no glossy 3D rendering
- no detailed skin rendering

Suggested colors:

- outline: `#30343B`
- hair / shirt: `#30343B` or nearby dark neutral
- skin: warm light neutral, compatible with `#FFFDF7`
- pendant accent: `#F2C94C` or muted gold near it

## 8. Pose library

The first approved character sheet should include at least:

1. neutral standing
2. thinking
3. sitting at a computer
4. reading / reviewing a document
5. holding a camera
6. holding a small electronics board or tool
7. pointing left
8. pointing right
9. presenting a result
10. mild confusion
11. realization
12. task complete / relaxed

These should become reusable transparent assets rather than being regenerated for every video.

## 9. Interaction with Q Cat

Wenhao and Q Cat may share scenes, but their roles differ:

- Wenhao represents the human narrator and decision-maker
- Q Cat represents curiosity, AI assistance, process state or a playful reaction

Do not make Q Cat visually dominate Wenhao in scenes where the human action is the subject.

## 10. Background and cutout production

For reusable assets:

- produce clean isolated poses with generous padding
- use a uniform key-color background when needed by the existing cutout workflow
- retain thin details such as glasses and pendant
- export final approved assets with real alpha transparency

## 11. Canonical approval package

Before this character is considered final, approve:

- front neutral master
- 3/4 neutral master
- side / partial-profile reference
- expression strip
- pose sheet
- color reference
- transparent PNG test at small and medium sizes

Recommended canonical filenames:

- `wenhao-master-front.png`
- `wenhao-master-34.png`
- `wenhao-expressions.png`
- `wenhao-pose-sheet.png`
- `wenhao-colors.md`

## 12. Never drift without approval

Do not silently change:

- glasses shape
- hair silhouette
- default dark top
- yellow/gold pendant accent
- body proportion family
- overall bright editorial illustration style

If a generator produces a visually attractive but different character, treat it as a rejected variation unless the master specification is intentionally revised.

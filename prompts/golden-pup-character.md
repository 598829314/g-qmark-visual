# Prompt — Golden Pup Mascot Master

Use this to refine the approved golden retriever puppy direction. Generated outputs remain references until explicitly approved.

## Fixed rules

- young golden retriever puppy
- compact round head and body
- short legs, floppy rounded ears
- cream muzzle and chest
- warm golden-ochre flat coat
- sleepy-friendly eyes
- minimal graphite editorial doodle lines
- same visual world as the small Wenhao stick figure

## Master-sheet prompt

```text
Create a clean mascot sheet for Golden Pup, the recurring golden retriever puppy in G_QMark Visual.
Draw a very young compact golden retriever with a slightly oversized round head, short legs, floppy rounded ears, broad cream muzzle, tiny dark nose, gentle sleepy-friendly eyes, a warm golden-ochre coat and a simplified curved tail.
Use minimal rounded slightly imperfect graphite outlines (#30343B), sparse flat fill and a warm off-white presentation background (#FFFDF7), matching the small Wenhao stick-figure doodle language.
Show consistent poses: sitting, lying with chin on paws, walking, carrying a soft ball, peeking from a folder, beside a laptop, watching progress, mild confusion, pleased and sleepy.
No cat features, pointy ears, border-collie markings, adult tall dog, realistic fur, anime eyes, clothing, robot parts, gradients, 3D, cyberpunk or text.
```

## Review

Reject if the mascot stops reading as a golden retriever puppy, becomes tall or realistic, accumulates fur detail, or drifts away from the small stick-figure line language.

# Prompt — Q Cat Mascot Master Exploration

Use this prompt to explore the first canonical Q Cat character sheet. Generated outputs are references until explicitly approved.

## Goal

Create a small recurring cat companion that can represent curiosity, AI assistance and lightweight process state without turning the whole visual system into children's illustration.

## Fixed identity rules

- compact rounded cat
- warm milk-gray body
- lighter belly / muzzle
- graphite outline
- small warm-yellow circular collar/tag accent
- tail naturally curves into a question-mark gesture
- calm, slightly curious default face
- simple enough to read at 32–64 px

## Character-sheet prompt

```text
Create a clean editorial mascot design sheet for a recurring cat named Q Cat.
Q Cat is a small compact rounded cat with a warm milk-gray body, a lighter cream belly and muzzle, simple dark graphite outlines (#30343B), tiny restrained facial features, and one small warm-yellow circular collar tag (#F2C94C).
Its strongest identifying silhouette is the tail: a natural thick cat tail that curves into a readable question-mark gesture without looking like a separate graphic symbol attached to the body.
The cat should feel calm, curious, friendly and lightly playful rather than hyperactive or childish.
Use softened triangular ears, compact paws, a short rounded body and a clear silhouette that still works at small icon size.
Use flat or lightly textured fills, minimal shadow and a warm off-white presentation background (#FFFDF7).
Show consistent views and expressions: front sitting neutral, side sitting with the question-mark tail clearly visible, walking, lying down, curious/look-up, thinking, confused, pleased, sleepy/waiting, and peeking from behind an object.
Keep body color, yellow circular accent, proportions, face style and tail concept consistent across every panel.
No robot parts, no antenna, no LED face, no exposed circuitry, no holograms, no cyberpunk, no glowing eyes, no huge anime eyes, no realistic fur rendering, no 3D, no rainbow palette, no elaborate clothing.
```

## Context pose follow-up

After a canonical Q Cat form is approved:

```text
Keep the approved Q Cat identity, proportions, milk-gray palette, yellow circular tag, face and question-mark tail unchanged.
Create isolated reusable poses of Q Cat: sitting beside a laptop, lying on a progress bar, peeking from a folder/window, sitting on a small NAS/local-model box, inspecting a model cube, sitting near a camera, watching a 3D printer/electronics project, reacting mildly to an error message, and accompanying Wenhao in an explainer scene.
Keep props simple and secondary. Leave generous padding around each pose for later transparent cutout use.
```

## Review checklist

Reject if:

- tail no longer reads as a natural question-mark gesture
- proportions become long, elegant or realistic
- cat becomes a robot mascot
- eyes become large anime eyes
- yellow accent becomes large or dominant
- extra colors accumulate without purpose
- the mascot stops working at small size

Prefer the direction with the clearest silhouette and the least visual complexity.

# Prompt — G? Logo Exploration

Use this prompt only for exploration before a canonical vector logo is approved. Do not promote any generated result into `assets/identity/` without review.

## Goal

Explore a compact personal identity mark for `G_QMark` built around the readable characters `G?`.

## Fixed identity rules

- The mark must read clearly as `G?`.
- Main strokes use dark graphite near `#30343B`.
- The question-mark dot uses warm yellow `#F2C94C`.
- The visual character is bright, calm, rounded, friendly and precise.
- The mark should work at 24 px and in simple SVG motion.
- Keep geometry simple enough to redraw deterministically as vector paths.

## Exploration prompt

```text
Design a compact flat vector-style identity mark for the personal visual system "G_QMark".
The primary mark must read clearly as "G?".
Use simple rounded geometric construction, clean sans-serif stroke logic, balanced spacing and an open readable G.
The G and question-mark stem are dark graphite (#30343B). The question-mark dot is a single warm-yellow circle (#F2C94C), functioning as the visual signature.
The result should feel bright, calm, friendly, precise and lightly playful, suitable for technical explainer videos, creative coding, electronics, photography and maker content.
Use a warm off-white background (#FFFDF7) for presentation only.
Keep the mark flat, minimal and easy to recreate as SVG paths.
No 3D, no gradients, no glow, no cyberpunk, no shield, no hexagon badge, no AI chip, no robot icon, no lightning bolt, no gaming aesthetic, no decorative circuitry, no mockup scene.
Present several clearly separated geometric directions while preserving the exact G? identity.
```

## Review checklist

Reject a direction if:

- `G?` is not immediately readable
- the yellow dot feels decorative rather than structural
- the mark depends on gradient, texture or 3D depth
- the geometry cannot be simplified into clean SVG
- it looks like a gaming, crypto, cybersecurity or generic AI logo

Prefer the direction that remains recognizable at very small size and can animate with a simple dot → question mark → G reveal.
